## Market Overview

Taiwan's technology sector is bifurcated: AI-infrastructure names (TSMC, Foxconn, Quanta, ASUS, Delta) are delivering exceptional revenue growth, while legacy semiconductor firms like MediaTek face cyclical softness. Financial sector reported revenues look distorted by IFRS 17 base effects and should be interpreted with caution. Shipping is normalizing after 2021–2022 freight supercycle highs.

## Company Snapshots

### Semiconductors

**台積電 TSMC (2330)** — Premium at P/E 32x and P/B 10.5x, but fully earned. April revenue NTD 411B (+17.5% YoY) with cumulative YTD growth of +30%. The world's leading advanced foundry continues to dominate AI chip manufacturing. Low dividend (0.92%) reflects reinvestment priority. Long-term hold / core position.

**聯電 UMC (2303)** — Reasonable P/E 31.4x for a mature foundry. Steady growth at +10.8% YoY, good 2.08% dividend. Mature node specialty (automotive, IoT, analog). Solid but unspectacular. Hold.

**聯發科 MediaTek (2454)** — 🚩 **MAJOR RED FLAG.** P/E of 70.6x and P/B of 18.1x are extreme while revenue is *declining* -4.1% YoY and -26% MoM. This is the most expensive stock in the basket with the worst fundamentals. The market is pricing in a recovery that hasn't materialized. Risk of significant de-rating.

### Technology Hardware

**鴻海 Foxconn (2317)** — The standout value play. P/E 20.8x and P/B 2.3x are cheap. April revenue NTD 832B (+29.7% YoY) — the highest absolute revenue in this basket. Cumulative YTD growth also +29.7%. Foxconn's AI server and Nvidia GB200 rack assembly business is driving massive growth. AI infrastructure buildout is a multi-year tailwind. Strong buy.

**廣達 Quanta (2382)** — Best growth/value ratio in the entire basket. P/E 20.3x with April revenue up a staggering +120.7% YoY. YTD cumulative growth +79.6%. Quanta is one of the world's largest AI server and cloud rack manufacturers, benefiting enormously from hyperscaler AI capex. At these multiples, extraordinary growth is priced cheaply. Strong buy.

**台達電 Delta Electronics (2308)** — 🚩 Expensive but with reason. P/E 89.4x, P/B 21x. April revenue NTD 58.7B (+43.9% YoY), YTD +36.5%. Delta's power management and thermal solutions are critical for AI data centers (high-density GPU racks need advanced power). Market is pricing in sustained high growth. Valuation leaves zero room for disappointment. Caution.

**華碩 ASUS (2357)** — Hidden gem. P/E 15.9x (cheapest tech hardware in group), P/B 2.3x, *and* 4.71% dividend yield (highest in tech group). April revenue NTD 81.9B (+45.7% YoY), YTD +42.4%. AI PC cycle + GPU board demand. Rare combination of growth + value + yield. Buy.

**大立光 LARGAN (3008)** — Niche camera lens leader. P/E 23.9x, P/B 2.7x. April +24.5% YoY, though YTD more moderate at +10.7%. Premium smartphone lens recovery. Hold/accumulate on weakness.

**瑞昱 Realtek (2379)** — IC design (networking, audio, storage). P/E 22.5x, 4% dividend. Modest growth +11.3% YoY. Solid but not exciting. Hold.

## Top Growth Opportunities 🚀

1. **廣達 Quanta (2382)** — AI server/rack manufacturing boom. Revenue +120.7% YoY at a P/E of only 20x. Best GARP opportunity in Taiwan market. Hyperscaler AI capex is multi-year.

2. **鴻海 Foxconn (2317)** — Nvidia GB200 rack assembly at scale. Revenue +29.7% YoY at P/E 20.8x and P/B 2.3x. Incredible value at this growth rate. AI infrastructure at scale.

3. **華碩 ASUS (2357)** — AI PC + GPU boards at a P/E of 15.9x with 4.71% dividend. Revenue +45.7% YoY. Chronically overlooked by international investors.

4. **台達電 Delta (2308)** — Power/thermal for AI data centers growing strongly. Risk is the valuation (P/E 89x), but the structural tailwind is real.

5. **台積電 TSMC (2330)** — The obvious AI play. Cumulative YTD revenue +30%. Indispensable for any AI chip. Core quality position.

---

## Key Risk Flags 🚩

1. **聯發科 MediaTek (2454) — Overvalued + Deteriorating.** P/E 70.6x while revenue is declining -4.1% YoY and -26% MoM. This is the most dangerous valuation trap in the basket. If smartphone/PC chip demand doesn't recover quickly, the stock faces severe multiple compression.

2. **台達電 Delta (2308) — Extreme valuation.** P/E 89.4x and P/B 21x mean a single bad quarter could cause 30–40% drawdown. Execution must be perfect.

3. **長榮 Evergreen (2603) — Freight cycle headwinds.** YTD revenue down 15.8%. Shipping rates post-pandemic normalization is ongoing. High dividend is at risk if earnings decline further.

4. **Financial sector data distortion.** The 577% and 1121% YoY revenue figures are not genuine growth — caused by accounting standard transitions (IFRS 17). Investors new to Taiwan financials may misinterpret these.

5. **MediaTek/Delta concentration risk.** Both are trading at extreme P/E multiples (70x and 89x). A tech sector rotation away from high-multiple names could cause outsized losses in these two positions.

## Investment Verdicts

### Strong Buy
- **廣達 Quanta (2382)** — AI server growth at a reasonable price. The P/E of 20x for 120% revenue growth is the most compelling setup in the basket.
- **鴻海 Foxconn (2317)** — Scale + AI server + cheap valuation. You get 30% revenue growth at the price of a mature industrial company.

### Buy
- **華碩 ASUS (2357)** — Cheap, growing fast, high dividend. Underappreciated AI PC/GPU play.
- **台積電 TSMC (2330)** — Quality premium is earned. AI chip indispensability means the moat grows wider every year.
- **兆豐金 Mega Financial (2886)** — Cheap financials with the best dividend among financials.

### Hold / Income
- **長榮 Evergreen (2603)** — Only for income-focused investors. 6.78% yield + below-book-value is a floor, but revenue headwinds are real.
- **中華電信 Chunghwa (2412)** — Defensive income, nothing more.
- **大立光 LARGAN (3008)**, **聯電 UMC (2303)**, **瑞昱 Realtek (2379)** — Solid businesses, adequate returns.

### Avoid
- **聯發科 MediaTek (2454)** — P/E 70x with declining revenue. The risk/reward is asymmetrically bad. If revenue doesn't recover sharply in H2 2026, the stock will re-rate harshly.
- **台達電 Delta (2308)** — Great business, terrible entry point at P/E 89x. Wait for a 30–40% correction before considering.
